How abs pop & muscles even out

  • Build the abs, don't cut lower — at ~10%/120lb more cutting just loses muscle. Train abs weighted, 2–3×/wk, near failure.
  • Even = balanced volume. ~10–20 hard sets per muscle per week, full range. Lagging part gets an extra set or two.
  • Eat slightly above maintenance, ~90–120g protein/day, to actually add visible muscle.
