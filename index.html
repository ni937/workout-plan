Is this plan good? Yes.

I checked your plan set by set. Every main muscle gets trained about twice a week. The weekly sets are in the range that builds muscle: chest ~14, back ~12, biceps ~9, triceps ~9, legs ~15. The rep ranges are right (8–12 for big lifts, 10–15 for small ones), and Sunday is always rest.

One optional tweak: broad shoulders are a top goal, but side delts only get 5 direct sets a week. Adding one more lateral-raise set on Thursday and Friday would help.

How to get stronger: pick a weight you can do with clean form, stopping 1–3 reps before you'd fail. When you hit the top of the rep range on every set (e.g. 12/12/12), go up one weight step next time.

Rest: 2–3 min on big lifts (presses, rows, pulldown, leg press, RDL), 1–2 min on small ones. Being sore isn't the goal; adding reps or weight is.

Food: about 250 calories over maintenance and 90–110 g protein a day. Aim to gain 0.25–0.5 lb a week. Sleep 7–9 hours.
